What is Postergism?

Postergism /post-erg-ism/

From the Latin post- (after) and Greek ergon (work).

noun — A socio-economic framework exploring how human society, governance, and individual purpose adapt when traditional work is no longer essential due to advanced automation and material abundance.

Postergism examines the shift beyond traditional labour-based value systems. As material scarcity fades, society must confront new measures of worth, inequality, and human meaning in a post-work world.

Featured Book

Postergism reimagines what an economy could look like when traditional jobs fade and society evolves. Through bold ideas and thought‑provoking scenarios, it challenges readers to see the future differently and rethink what truly drives progress.
